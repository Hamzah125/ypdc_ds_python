# OOP is a concept that revolves around classes and objects
It has many benifits, Reusabilty, easy maintenance, Scalability , 
Modularity

CLASSES: IS like a blueprint e.g: cars 4 wheeler, windscreeen, engine 
object: actual instance e.g Corolla 1988
Inheritence: inheriting properties from parent class: F1: 1 seater
Encapsulation : Bind data & functions in 1 unit and hide internal details 
Polymorphism: same method name; deifferent behavior depending on objects
Abstraction: hiding unneccesary details , present only necessary
